Social App Analytics · Privacy · Terms

Privacy policy

Updated September 30, 2026. This policy describes the planned integration; collection has not started.

Social App Analytics is a private TikTok analytics tool operated by the owner of the connected accounts. Questions or deletion requests: getripplenews@gmail.com.

Information and purpose

The planned integration collects authorized account identifiers, display information, account statistics, public video identifiers, captions, titles, publication times, links, and available view, like, comment and share counts. Historical snapshots and manually supplied content tags support analysis and future content planning.

Authorization and storage

Authorization takes place with TikTok. The tool does not request or store TikTok passwords. OAuth access and refresh tokens will be stored separately from analytics records and excluded from analytics exports. Analytics are intended to remain in local storage on the operator’s Mac.

AI analysis

Selected analytics may be supplied to Claude Code for content planning. When sent to an external AI service, those selected data are processed under that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Tokens, passwords and private notes are excluded from the reader export.

Retention and control

Historical analytics are retained until the operator deletes them or ends use of the tool. Connected accounts can revoke authorization through TikTok; revocation stops authorized API access but does not itself remove earlier snapshots. Contact the operator to request deletion of locally stored records and exports. Data already sent to an external provider are subject to that provider’s retention controls.

Public website

The proposed public site contains informational pages only. It has no analytics trackers or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
